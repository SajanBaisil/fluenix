What changed vs the Stitch draft

  • Home has one job now. The draft stacked level card + metrics + sessions + CTA; here the daily-goal ring is the call button — everything else is secondary.
  • Stock photos → gradient monograms. AI-photo coaches look uncanny and inconsistent; monograms stay on-brand at every size and cost nothing.
  • One ring language. Goal ring, report score, progress chart all share the same indigo→violet stroke, so "progress" looks the same everywhere.
  • Corrections follow one pattern: red-struck "you said" → green "say this" → one-line why. Same component in report, paywall teaser, and live-call chip.
  • Two screens the draft didn't have: the paywall (report teaser is the conversion moment) and onboarding.
